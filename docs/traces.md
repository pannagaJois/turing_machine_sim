## TM 1 — Addition: 111+11

| Step | State | Tape (head in [ ]) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | q0 | `[1]11+11` |
| 1 | q0 | `1[1]1+11` |
| 2 | q0 | `11[1]+11` |
| 3 | q0 | `111[+]11` |
| 4 | q1 | `1111[1]1` |
| 5 | q1 | `11111[1]` |
| 6 | q1 | `111111[_]` |
| 7 | q2 | `11111[1]` |
| 8 | qaccept | `11111_[_]` |

Steps 0–3: q0 scans right over the first block until it reads `+`. The step 3 → 4
transition overwrites `+` with `1`, merging the blocks into 6 ones. Steps 4–6: q1
runs right to the blank. Step 6 → 7: step back onto the last `1`. Step 7 → 8:
erase it. Result: `11111` = 5 in unary = 3 + 2, in **8 steps**. Correct.
